Osmosis lab notes
Thursday, Room 214

Setup
- Three beakers: 0% (distilled), 0.9%, and 5% NaCl.
- Potato cores, 3 cm long, weighed before soaking.

Observations after 30 minutes
- Distilled water: cores firm, mass up about 8%.
- 0.9% saline: almost no change.
- 5% saline: cores limp, mass down about 11%.

Questions for the report
1. Which solution was isotonic to the potato cells?
2. Why did the 5% cores lose mass?
3. What would happen to a red blood cell in each beaker?
